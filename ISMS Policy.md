**1\. Purpose**

This policy states the commitment of SoSecureSwitch Ltd. to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its information assets, including client data and its proprietary intellectual property, to manage risks in line with business objectives.

**2\. ISMS Objectives**

SoSecureSwitch Ltd. is committed to:

1. **Protecting the SoSecureSwitch product as a primary commercial asset (IP)** by applying appropriate organisational, technical and contractual controls to preserve its confidentiality, integrity and availability. (supports Annex A: 5.33)  
2. **Operating a risk-based ISMS** in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 that identifies, assesses and treats risks to in-scope assets and services, with documented risk owner assignments and treatment plans. (supports Clause 6 planning)  
3. **Meeting legal, regulatory and contractual obligations** including applicable banking regulations, data-protection law and customer SLAs; providing timely notifications and audit evidence to customers and regulators as required. (supports Annex A: 5.32–5.36 & 5.24–5.26)  
4. **Maintaining business continuity and resilience** for critical transaction services, including verified backup/restore and DR capability for production systems and source-code repositories. (supports Annex A: 5.29–5.31)  
5. **Applying least privilege and robust privileged access controls** for administering production systems, HSMs, and source repositories including MFA, PAM where required and documented break-glass procedures. (supports Annex A: 5.15–5.18)  
6. **Ensuring secure development and release integrity**, including secure SDLC practices, code review, automated testing, signed build artifacts and gating of production releases. (supports Annex A: 8.25–8.29; 8.24–8.27)  
7. **Operating detection and response capabilities** (logging, monitoring, IR runbooks, tabletop exercises) to minimize MTTD and MTTR and to provide auditable evidence to customers and regulators. (supports Annex A: 8.7–8.11; 5.24–5.26)  
8. **Managing supplier and third-party risk** by requiring contractual security obligations (NDAs, IP assignment clauses, support controls) for suppliers that access or support in-scope systems. (supports Annex A: 5.19–5.23)  
9. **Investing in people and awareness**, ensuring personnel are screened appropriately, receive role-based security training, and follow on-boarding/off-boarding controls that protect IP and transaction systems. (supports Clause 7.2, 7.3 and Annex A: 6.1-6.3)  
10. **Driving continual improvement** by measuring ISMS performance, conducting internal audits and management reviews, and closing corrective actions in a timely and tracked manner. (supports Clause 9 & 10\)

**3\. Scope**

This policy applies to all information, systems, processes, and personnel within the defined scope of the ISMS, as documented in the ISMS Scope Statement (SSS-DOC-ISMS-003).

**4\. Roles & Responsibilities**

**CEO (ISMS Sponsor):** approves and funds the ISMS, reviews management reports, signs the policy, ensures management engagement.

**CTO / ISMS Owner:** maintains the ISMS, ensures implementation of this policy, owns the SoA and evidence index, reports to CEO.

**Product Owner / Dev Manager:** responsible for secure SDLC, asset classification for product components, and maintaining CI/CD evidence.

**IT Ops Lead / Build & Release Engineer:** responsible for operation, HSM custody, backup/restore tests and production availability.

**Information Security Manager:** day-to-day ISMS coordination, risk assessments, internal audits and security awareness.

**HR, Legal, Procurement:** implement HR security (screening, training), legal/IP contract obligations and supplier due diligence respectively.

**All employees and contractors:** adhere to this policy, attend required training, report incidents promptly, and follow access/use rules.

**5\. Information Security Objectives (measurable)**

Top management commits to the following measurable objectives; owners and targets are recorded in the ISMS objectives register:

1. **SLA Compliance:** ≥99.9% availability for production transaction switch (measured monthly).  
2. **MTTD:** Reduce Mean Time To Detect to \< 30 minutes for P1 security events (measured quarterly).  
3. **MTTR:** Reduce Mean Time To Respond to P1 incidents to \< 4 hours (measured quarterly).  
4. **Patch SLA:** ≥95% of critical patches applied to in-scope servers within 14 days of release (measured monthly).  
5. **Evidence Coverage:** ≥90% of Annex A controls marked Applicable in SoA must have at least one linked evidence artifact uploaded to the evidence index (measured quarterly).  
6. **Training:** 100% of in-scope staff complete role-based security training annually.  
7. **Privileged Access:** 100% privileged account reviews completed quarterly; time to revoke access for leavers ≤ 24 hours.

**6\. Commitment to Continual Improvement**

SoSecureSwitch Ltd. is committed to the continual review and improvement of the ISMS, in accordance with ISO 27001:2022, to enhance its effectiveness and adapt to changing business needs and threats. This is achieved through regular audits, management reviews, and risk assessments.
