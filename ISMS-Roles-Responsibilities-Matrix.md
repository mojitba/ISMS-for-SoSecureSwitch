| **Role**                              | **Responsibilities**                                                                                                                   | **Authority**                                                                     | **Evidence of Competence**                                  |
| :------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CEO**                               | Approve ISMS Policy, allocate resources, ensure ISMS objectives align with business strategy, and chair management reviews.            | Full organizational authority over ISMS resources and decisions.                  | Signed ISMS Policy, Management Review Minutes               |
| **CTO**                               | Ensure technical compliance, assign control and asset owners, approve risk treatment plans, and oversee ISMS implementation.           | Technical and operational authority for information systems and ISMS initiatives. | Risk Treatment Plan approvals, technical strategy documents |
| **Deputy CEO**                        | Support CEO in ISMS oversight, ensure communication with banking clients on ISMS matters, manage business continuity planning.         | Delegated authority in CEO’s absence for ISMS decisions.                          | Continuity Plan, customer communication logs                |
| **Board Director**                    | Provide strategic direction, approve ISMS funding and objectives, review ISMS performance reports.                                     | Strategic oversight authority.                                                    | Board meeting minutes                                       |
| **ISMS Manager**                      | Maintain ISMS documentation, coordinate internal/external audits, manage risk register, and report ISMS performance to top management. | Operational authority to update and enforce ISMS procedures.                      | ISMS Manual, Internal Audit Reports                         |
| **Information Security Officer**      | Monitor control implementation, perform security assessments, manage incident response, and deliver security awareness training.       | Authority to escalate security incidents and enforce control remediation.         | Incident logs, awareness training records                   |
| **IT Operations Lead**                | Implement and monitor infrastructure security controls (patching, backups, DR, network hardening).                                     | Authority over system configurations and network operations.                      | Patch reports, backup verification logs                     |
| **System Administrator**              | Maintain server configurations, access controls, and OS updates in line with ISMS requirements.                                        | Administrative privileges for system-level operations.                            | Configuration baselines, access logs                        |
| **Developer Manager**                 | Ensure secure development lifecycle, control access to source repositories, maintain CI/CD pipeline integrity.                         | Authority over development lifecycle and release approvals.                       | Dev policies, release management logs                       |
| **Developers**                        | Follow secure coding standards, maintain confidentiality of source code, and promptly report vulnerabilities.                          | Limited authority within assigned projects.                                       | Code review records, training logs                          |
| **HR Manager**                        | Conduct background checks, enforce HR-related security policies, manage onboarding/offboarding and training records.                   | HR administrative authority for all employees.                                    | Personnel records, training registers                       |
| **Legal Counsel**                     | Review and approve contracts, NDAs, and supplier agreements to ensure inclusion of information-security clauses.                       | Authority to approve or reject contracts and NDAs.                                | Supplier contracts, NDA repository                          |
| **Procurement Officer**               | Ensure supplier evaluations include security requirements, maintain supplier risk register, verify SLAs.                               | Authority to engage and monitor suppliers.                                        | Supplier evaluations, risk register                         |
| **Backup & DR Coordinator**           | Manage backup schedules, perform DR tests, verify restore integrity, maintain related documentation.                                   | Operational authority over backup and DR systems.                                 | Backup logs, DR test reports                                |
| **Network & Infrastructure Engineer** | Maintain firewalls, routers, load balancers, and network security; perform periodic configuration reviews.                             | Authority to configure and maintain network devices.                              | Firewall change logs, configuration audits                  |
| **SOC / Monitoring Engineer**         | Monitor SIEM alerts, manage event triage, ensure log integrity, and escalate confirmed incidents.                                      | Authority to trigger incident response and notify stakeholders.                   | SIEM configuration, incident tickets                        |
| **DBA**                               | Maintain transaction databases, implement backup and encryption policies, ensure data integrity and security baselines.                | Administrative authority over databases.                                          | Hardening checklists, backup records                        |
| **Asset Owners**                      | Protect and properly classify assigned assets, maintain access records, and ensure compliance with ISMS controls.                      | Operational authority over assigned assets.                                       | Asset register, access reviews                              |
| **Risk Owners**                       | Approve risk treatment actions, monitor residual risks, and report progress to the ISMS Manager.                                       | Decision-making authority for assigned risks.                                     | Risk register, treatment records                            |
| **All Employees**                     | Comply with ISMS policies, report information-security incidents, and complete required awareness training.                            | No management authority (responsibility only).                                    | Training records, policy acknowledgements                   |
